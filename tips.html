Gardening Tips

Helpful advice from Norway Nursery to keep your landscape healthy all year long.

Water Deeply

Water plants early in the morning and deeply rather than lightly every day. Deep watering encourages stronger root growth and healthier plants.

Mulch Your Beds

Apply 2–3 inches of mulch around flowers, shrubs, and trees to retain moisture, reduce weeds, and regulate soil temperature.

Plant the Right Plant in the Right Place

Always consider sunlight, soil type, and available space before planting. Choosing the proper location helps plants thrive with less maintenance.

Fertilize Properly

Feed plants during their active growing season using the appropriate fertilizer. Avoid over-fertilizing, which can damage roots and reduce flowering.

Prune at the Right Time

Remove dead or damaged branches regularly. Most flowering shrubs should be pruned after blooming, while many trees are best pruned during dormancy.

Watch for Pests

Inspect leaves and stems frequently for insects or disease. Early detection makes treatment easier and helps prevent damage.
