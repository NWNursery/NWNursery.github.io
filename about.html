About Norway Nursery

Welcome to Norway Nursery! We are a family-owned nursery dedicated to providing high-quality plants, trees, and landscaping products to our local community.

Located in Marseilles, Illinois, we take pride in offering healthy, beautiful plants along with friendly service and expert knowledge to help customers create their perfect outdoor spaces.

At Norway Nursery, our goal is simple: to provide quality plants, excellent customer service, and a welcoming experience for every gardener and homeowner.
